AQUARIUM SURVIVAL GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY

Take care of your creature and keep its aquarium healthy. Maintain a suitable environment to help it survive.

🌡️ Temperature

Adjust the aquarium temperature to maintain a suitable environment.

🐟 Feed

Give your creature food to reduce its hunger.

🧹 Clean

Clean the aquarium to maintain water cleanliness.

🧸 Toy

Provide toys and enrichment for your creature.

💡 Light

Turn the aquarium lighting on or off.

⏸️ Pause

Pause or resume the aquarium simulation.

⏩ Game Speed

Choose between 1×, 2× and 4× simulation speed.

💾 Save

Save your current aquarium progress.
